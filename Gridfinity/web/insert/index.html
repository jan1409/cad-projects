Gridfinity Tool Insert

Photograph your tools on a sheet of paper and get a Gridfinity box with a pocket for each of them.

1Photo
How to take the photo
  • Lay the sheet on a dark, plain surface and the tools on the sheet, at least 1 cm from its edges.
  • Shoot straight from above, from at least 50 cm, zoomed in (2×) so the sheet fills the picture.
  • Use even, diffuse light and no flash: hard shadows make the outline larger on one side.
  • Shiny metal can reflect like paper: check the outline and correct it in the layout.
